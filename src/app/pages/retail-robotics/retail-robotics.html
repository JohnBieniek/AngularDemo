Retail Robotics & Planogram Mapping

The Home Depot | Zippedi integration

I developed the integration and analysis layer that combined Zippedi shelf imagery and robot-location data with The Home Depot's store layouts and planograms. The system placed each observation within the correct aisle, bay, shelf, and vertical position, compared expected labels and products with the captured image, and produced usable information about shelf condition, product placement, stock availability, and discrepancies.

The problem

A shelf image only became useful once it could be located and understood.

Zippedi supplied shelf imagery and the robot's location. The Home Depot's store-layout data described the aisle, bay, and shelf, while planogram data described the labels and products expected at each position. Those representations did not identify the same physical shelf in the same way.

The challenge was to reconcile the robot's position with the store model, determine how high on the shelf the camera was looking, and align the expected labels and products with what was actually visible. Only then could an image describe the state of a real shelf.

My contribution

From robot location to actionable shelf intelligence.

  • Matched the robot's reported location to the correct aisle, bay, and shelf.
  • Resolved the vertical shelf position represented in each captured image.
  • Connected that location to the planogram's expected labels and products.
  • Compared expected labels with visible labels and identified the product above each one.
  • Converted the matched observations into structured shelf, label, stock, and discrepancy information.
